Mary's Diaries: the Digital Edition

The diaries kept by Mary Berenson between 1891 and 1937 are preserved in the Biblioteca Berenson at I Tatti - the Harvard University Center for Italian Renaissance Studies in Florence.

They not only contain descriptions of Mary's many visits with Bernard to museums, churches, and private collections; but also reveal her thoughts on books, music, and the myriad people the couple encountered over the years.

Mary's diaries capture the zeitgeist of the Berensons' I Tatti, where the guests at afternoon tea were nearly as distinguished as the Renaissance artists the Villa's occupants spent their mornings researching. Mary's casual reference to a young "Keynes", for example, was none other than John Maynard Keynes, while "Bertie" was simply an affectionate nickname for Bertrand Russell.

The diaries form a potentially important source for all manner of research projects and as such, the Biblioteca Berenson has decided to make them available in an online edition.
