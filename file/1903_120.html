In the evening we dined with Clyde Fitch,38 who had the actress Miss Elsie de Wolfe and Miss Marbury. A rather jolly evening. Miss de Wolfe told us many stories about Gladys, with whom she spent the summer at Versailles.

Bernhard made a ‘mot’ in Herford’s vein at the lunch, when they were discussing the difference between a nuisance and a convenience and he said, A nuisance is another mans’ convenience.
