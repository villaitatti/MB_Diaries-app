At dinner we talked of Loeser, with whom Maud had gone to look at the drawings, returning quite furious with his ignorance and conceit and inappropriateness. She agreed that he always finds, as Bernhard says, “le mot injuste”. He called Pisanello “monumental and solemn” - !! Their final split came over the Mantegna Judith, which he thinks is not genuine. Bernhard is reading Karl Pearson’s Grammar of Science, and finds it a splendid piece of clear thinking, and the most destructive to Catholicism that there has yet been. I must remember this for Ray and Karin.
