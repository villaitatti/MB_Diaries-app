A Miss Harver played to us – as agreeably as if she were an organ grinder – and then she and Lina Duff-Gordon came out to walk with us through the Vincigliata woods. The weather is heavenly – irises already out, and daffodils everywhere. In the evening we began I Kings. We were much shocked by David’s death-bed!

Thursday Feb. 3. 1898. Frullino. Florence.

I had my music lesson – Buonamici played Beethoven to me. Miss Blood called and raged because we were having the cypresses carelessly planted. Janet came up to stay for a few days. We went on with I Kings.
