Brought up by a mamma who thinks of nothing but Dress and Sex, her mind plays around all the problems of sex in a most alluring manner, with an audacity and outspokenness that make your hair stand on end. She is positively impish. But she has never felt anything, so she dares.

Her defects are bad form - for she is distinctly in bad form - and lying; but as Bernhard says, she is so wonderful, she can afford the first, and she may outgrow the second.

Friday May 9. 1902., I Tatti, Settignano

It has been rainy and cold all day - thermometer at 60º. We went over Bernhard’s chapter on Andrea. Really, he has written a Great Book! We called on the Rosses, too, who are missing Lina - though Lina is doubtless so busy in London with preparations for her wedding, that she doesn’t think of her old life.

Gladys writes from Blenheim: “The sniffy Oxford Dons are very displeased at the prospect of having to receive the holders of the Rhodes scholarships, and are grumbling that it is going to cause great expense to the University. Among the political men I have seen there seems to prevail little else than jealousy, and it is only among the upper and the upper middle classes that unstinted praise is given to the Great Man.”
