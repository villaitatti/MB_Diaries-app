Strolled along the Boulevard in Park on old town wall, and came to the Cathedral. Went inside, good proportions, light and gay, with beautiful stained glass. Lets in the real god, the Sun. We saw the treasures: Hair of Blessed Virgin, Spike from Crown of Thorns, Several inches of wood from True Cross (set in gold and jewels, which having been pawned to the Jews of Regensburg, was seized from them and deposited there), brown mummified hand of S. Chrysostom with diamond ring; Hand of Innocent massacred at Bethlehem; Hordes of Bones, etc.; Skeleton of Child in jewels, etc. “found under floor of Jewish Synagogue after the expulsion of the Jews from Regensburg in the XV Century/” (!)

Drove out to the Walhalla (there was a steam tram) which was startling and surprising. We walked up through a pine forest and came suddenly upon the white Doric columns of the temple flashing in the sunlight.
