Friday Jan. 21.98.

Jenkins came to lunch and we chatted pleasantly. Bernhard went to the concert of the Bolognese Quartette. Miss Blood and the Princess Ghika came over to advise me about my garden, and I was very grateful. Their suggestions were admirable.
