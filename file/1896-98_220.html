Friday Feb. 18. 1898. Frullino. Florence

Bicycling. Began Psalms. Finished vol. 1 “Golden Bough.” Lunched with Rosses and had a nice walk.

Saturday Feb. 19.98.

Kerr-Lawson to lunch. Bicycling.

Sunday Feb. 20. 1898.

Horne to lunch. Trevy and Bernhard called on Miss Blood. I called on the Mortons – who were ill, as usual, poor things.

Monday Feb. 21.98.

The Benns and Mr. Morgan came to lunch. Psalms.
