After lunch we went to the Capello [sic] di San Giorgio and enjoyed the wonderful Altichieris, in spite of the bitter, piercing cold, which suddenly took the place of the fine, mild weather.

The last part of the afternoon we spent in the Eremitani before the Mantegna frescoes.

B. was awfully sick in the night.

Friday, October 30, 1891, Padua
