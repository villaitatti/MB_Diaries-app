Friday March 12. [i.e. 11.] 1898. Frullino. Florence.

Logan suddenly decided to go to London, so we drove down and saw him off, and then I took Trevy to the Pitti and called on Lady Lewis and Mrs. Horne. Bernhard and Trevy had tea with Benn.

Saturday March 13. [i.e. 12.] 1898.

All three lunched at Poggio Gherardo, and then saw over the “Canovaia”, which Trevy thinks of taking. In the evening we read his Daedalus epic.

Sunday March 14. [i.e. 13.]98.

Horne came to lunch and we walked out to the Tree. Horne said Pater had very bad taste in music. The Kerr-Lawsons called. Read Trevy’s poetry in the evening.
