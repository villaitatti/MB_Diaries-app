Walking home we met two carabinieri in the moonlight fleeing down hill in search of a man who had been stabbed in a fray, and who was lying in a house at the top of the hill (as they very well knew!). We said we had not seen or heard any disturbance our way, whereupon they turned and fled up hill in their huge three-cornered hats and flapping black cloaks. It looked very weird. In a moment we came to where the ground was spattered with blood, and presently to the house where the wounded man lay, with the “chorus” hanging round the steps, the shrill choked voices of women inside. It was a case of two rival bands. A giovane in one taunted another in the rival band with having too small a mouth to blow his horn.
