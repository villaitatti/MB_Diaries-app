Wednesday Feb. 23.98.

Third music lesson of Mme. de Platonoff. She is a very good teacher. Called on Hornes.

Thursday Feb. 24.98.

Too rainy to start on our trip. All four went to Bargello. Then Bernhard and I called on Hornes. Psalms.

Friday Feb. 25.98.

Quiet day of practising. Called at Rosses.
