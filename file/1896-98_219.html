Wednesday Feb. 16. 1898.

Bernhard spent the day with Horne, driving out to see pictures etc. I had a music-lesson – a most satisfactory one. Maud Cruttwell came to lunch and we discussed the precise meaning of a “cultivated” person. Read Job and selected “Sacred” pictures in the evening.

Thursday Feb. 17. 1898.

Bernhard showed me a dear little Benvenuto di Giovanni he bought. Kerr-Lawson came to lunch and Bernhard sat to him while I went to Buonamici for a music-lesson. Called on Mme. Rasponi, who is ill. Finished Job in the evening and discussed the “moral view of life.”
