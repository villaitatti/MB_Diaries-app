At 2.15 we stopped at Orange, and after a quarrel over the Roman Arch, which Bernhard found more solid and grander than I did, we proceeded to be overwhelmed by the imposing façade of the Theatre. After exhausting that emotion, we ate some fruit, walked out to see a glorious sunset on Mt. Ventoux, smoked, and then took the train here.

Friday, October 4, 1895, Avignon

The thing to do would be to get rooms at the quiet Europe, and eat here, for the restaurant is not bad.

We spent a really rapturous day seeing this town and Villeneuve opposite, with its marvellous views.

The Galleries are complete frauds, except for a small mask by Laurana.
