Sunday March 6.’98. Centrale. San Gimignano

A wonderful spring day. We explored Volterra, and found two more “sacred” pictures – as well as most lovely views. In the afternoon we bicycled – drove here – on very beautiful, but rather difficult road. Herbert Horne was waiting for us, and a nice dinner was prepared.
