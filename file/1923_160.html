Wednesday, May 30, 1923

Athens

Fine

All day motoring from Delphi to Athens over Thebes. We had our lunch by the Cheronean [sic] Lion and tea overlooking the first of the 3 great Beoetian plains sloping up to Mt. Cithaeron behind which towered Parnassus. The road to Bralo [sic] is splendid and very beautiful. From Livadia [sic] to Thebes it is AWFUL!!
