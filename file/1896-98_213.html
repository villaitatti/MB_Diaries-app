Friday Feb. 4. 1898. Frullino.

Mr. and Mrs. Scott-Barber came to lunch. They were rather nice, and she was very amusing. He gave us some music – Bach, Scarlatti, Chopin and a quaint little old pavane.

Saturday Feb. 5. 1898.

Finished my review (for Atlantic) of “Central Italians”. Janet was ill. We went to Buonamici’s concert and then to see the newly discovered Ghirlandaio in the Ognissanti. Began II Kings. Dolmetsch has telegraphed for Janet to go and take the place of Elodie who is ill. He will himself give her harpsichord lessons. It is really a great chance for her – if only she is well enough to avail herself of it.
