Sunday March 20.98. Frullino Florence

Horne lunch here, and Mr. Scudder (editor Atlantic) called – a respectable old American. Horne’s article in the Saturday, inspired by Bernhard, attacking Poynter as Director of the National Gallery, is making a great stir in London. In the evening we worked on lists. Began Rabelais.

Monday Mar. 21.98.

[Kerr-]Lawson, Horne and the Halseys to lunch. The latter bored me so that I was desperate, until Bernhard suggested our driving to Brozzi to look up a reputed Botticini. This we did, and found 2, and other pictures beside, and I was somewhat consoled.
