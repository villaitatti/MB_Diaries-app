Sunday Jan. 16. 1898. Frullino. Florence

Last touches – really last – to our houses. Horne came to lunch – quiet and cautious as usual, but sympathetic. Afterwards came the Morgan family, Mr. and Mrs. Kerr-Lawson and Mr. and Mrs. Morton – a tiring, tiresome group. Logan and Bernhard escaped and went to the Gamberaia, but the Morgans stayed and stayed. When they at last went, I walked home with the Kerr-Lawsons and saw a really charming portrait of Maurice Hewlitt’s boy he is painting.

In the evening we read ten chapters of Samuel.
