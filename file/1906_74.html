Monday, Apr. 30, 1906

Pouring.

Emily, Britten, Logan.

B.B. at Venice.

Hapgoods to dine — so nice.

Tuesday, May 1, 1906

Pouring.

B.B. at Venice. Miss Isan and Miss Langton to lunch.

Called with Logan on the Huttons.

Wednesday, May 2, 1906

Pouring then fine.

B.B. at Verona.

Drove with Logan and Dan Randi to see a Villa at Arcetri.
