3 Somerset Hotel. Boston Shopped, Bernhard having a “treatment” from Mrs. Longyear. I lunched with Mrs. Tyler, who had 5 very middle class dull ladies to meet me, - a very good lunch. B.B. had Mr. Bullard to lunch. I paid calls, including one on Mrs. Elliott and her dear old mother Julia Ward Howe, also one on Mrs. Higginson and a Mrs. Rogers. In the evening we went to Savin Hill

3. Guernsey Hall, Princeton New Jersey

Spent the day coming here. The rule in the Pullman cars is 71º of heat, but it is generally nearer 85º. The view of New York from the Ferry - like a Cyclopean San Gemignano - is very fine. Mrs. Marquand is pretty and intelligent, Mrs. Marquand

strain keeps him constantly worried and anxious and uncomfortably pinched. I think his father ought to be ashamed to not even try to do anything to help out. The others are as nice about it as they can be, but there it is. And if Senda and Bessie break down in health, as they probably [will], he will have them, and Senda is a young lady of very luxurious tastes that one hates to say no to. She and Ray are the nice ones - we really do not care whether we see the others again or not, although we wish them well, and Bernhard will always do the right thing by them.
