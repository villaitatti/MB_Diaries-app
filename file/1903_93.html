We got home about 5 and rested and then went to dine with the Robinsons, who had no other guests. They told us how Dick Norton sold Mrs. Montgomery Sears some forged “Etruscan” jewelry. [sic] Mrs. Sears brought it to the Museum and insisted on knowing what they thought; so at last they had to tell her.
