Power and Fox and Billy Taylor went to see Bernhard in bed. I went again to the Whistlers with Mr. Bullard, and brought him back to lunch. I spent the afternoon at Mrs. Gardner’s, where the fashionable Vincent Club of girls gave a Vaudeville performance. Dined with Longyears, I alone.

Somerset Hotel, Boston, Saturday, Feb. 27, 1904

Bernhard rather better. His mother and Rachel came to lunch, and then came with me to Wellesley where I lectured on “The New Art Criticism” — one of my best lectures, by chance, this time.

Bernhard went to bed, but I dined with Mrs. Nickerson, pleasant, but too noisy dinner.
