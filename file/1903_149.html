Mrs. Caton and I winked at each other, for we saw very well that she meant to say she had drunk the cup at the full, and we were having only the dregs!

They had roaring time, only interrupted by Mrs. MacVeagh’s desire to speak evil in Bernhard’s ear of Mrs. Gardner, who sat just opposite, with two enormous diamonds fastened to her head on quivering gold stems. Mrs. Gardner grew so jealous of B. B.’s flirtation with Mrs. Caton that after dinner she told all the ladies how she had known B.
