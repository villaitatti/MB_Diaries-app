“Vernon Lee” talked nonsense about “literature” all the evening, and I felt rather sick. She denies the possibility of historical ideas, and says that literature has nothing to do with fact, i.e., that if Pater had written a Plato founded upon spurious inferior neo-Platonic writings, the book would have had an equal value! which is — in my opinion — standing on one’s head.

Saturday, March 31, 1894, Maiano

Talked, drove and looked at a villa.

Talked, met Mr. Benn, read Francis Thompson’s poems with Mr. Hamilton.
