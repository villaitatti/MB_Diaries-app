Friday, June 12, 1908

I Tatti.

Fine.

Massage.

Did Naples notes — very complicated.

Slept after lunch.

Cannon called to say goodbye — very dull.

Dined at Gamberaia with the Countess Serristori and Placci. It was wonderfully beautiful, but we all seemed to me very banal.
