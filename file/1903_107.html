We are invited to go to a strange Settlement in the Catskill Mountains. The founder writes “Our enterprise here is in its infancy, and we have not yet got to work to produce anything. I think a Community nourished on Dancing and Decoration is really original.

The man’s name is Ralph Radcliffe Whitehead.
