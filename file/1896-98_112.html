Monday April 12. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.

Mr. Benn and Mr. Blaydes came to lunch. Benn told us about the ideas and ideals of his youth in the sixties – Mill, George Eliot, and Tennyson, “St. Paul’s for architecture and Schiller for the drama.” Afterwards we took a walk, and then I went down and met Christina Bremner, who arrived soon after 7.

Tuesday April 13. 1897.

Perry came to tea. I called on Mrs. Halsey, and saw the lawyer.
