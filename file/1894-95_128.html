Stoeving I liked, but I have no vitality to spare to make new friends I care about. And then the gulf that separates a German —! You think they are delicate and refined and sensitive (like HO), and so they are in some respects, and then they are capable of throwing it all overboard and wallowing. We others make at any rate a faint endeavour not to be pigs anywhere, but Germans, as Bernhard says, do not seem to feel any “responsibility toward small things”.
