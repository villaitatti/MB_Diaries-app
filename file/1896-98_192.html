Wednesday Jan. 5.98. Frullino. San Domenico

Began on Bernhard’s ’s house – it seems hopeless. All in confusion here, too.

Thursday Jan. 6. 1898.

Moved Bernhard’s furniture and began to put up hangings. His bed-room is a dream – painted silk hangings, matting, white fur, and almost Chippendale furniture.

Friday Jan. 7.98.

Order coming slowly. More eggs removed from the calorifer! Bernhard lunched with Father Fox.
