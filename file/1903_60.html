Then Mrs. Pringsheim called, and then, oh then, Mrs. Longyear, the millionaire Christian Scientist. She is very Franciscan in her ecstatic love of Humanity and individuals, in her contentment with (what she thinks are) God’s ways, and in her hatred of books. “What do I want of books?” she cried. “I know.” She spoke continually of herself and God as one — it was like 2 + 2 = 4,and sin and disease and death along with God and herself are like 2 + 2 = 5.
