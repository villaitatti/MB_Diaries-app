Thursday Sept. 2. 1897. Mengelle. Brussels

I arrived this morning and found Bernhard and his sister, come up from St. Moritz, where they had been spending a month in the midst of fashionable and fascinating Roman and Neapolitan princes and princesses. Bernhard was captivated by the Duchessa Grazioli…We saw the Cathedral and the Museum and had a drive in the Bois. Impressions much the same as last year.

Friday Sept. 3. 1897. Grand Laboureur. Antwerp.

Came here – saw Cathedral and Museum – same impressions as before. Shopped. Senda remarkably beautiful and refreshingly simple.

Saturday Sept. 4. 1897. Union. Aachen

Saw Senda off at 7, and then did the two small museums – Plantin and Kumms. Disappointing. Came here and saw the Cathedral.
