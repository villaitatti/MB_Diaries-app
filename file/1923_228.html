Friday, August 3, 1923

Bad Gastein

Downpours

Reached Schwarzach at 3 and took train at 5 to come here. The manager said we had given up our rooms, and he had nothing for us. I waited most uncomfortably for an hour till he found the letter, it was from a man named Bensingu. He was full of apologies, and did find me a small room and promised a big one next for B.B. tomorrow.
