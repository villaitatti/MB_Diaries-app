I came home, dressed, received a call from Mrs. George Carpenter and went to Mrs. Manson’s reception, when I met Cora Burr Harden, who walked back with me to have the “honour” of being introduced to Bernhard.

Mr. Laffan, of the Sun, was here, he paid a call of 1 1.2 hours. Why? Norman says he is as sharp as a steel trap, and never wastes his time.
