Monday Feb. 7.98.

A long and boring afternoon choosing bicycles. Alfred Austin and his wife called on Bernhard. Janet better. Finished II Kings.

Tuesday Feb. 8.98.

Miss Duff-Gordon and Mrs. Johnson came to lunch and Bernhard took them (with Logan) to the Academy, while I sat to Kerr-Lawson for my portrait. Janet went away. Mrs Horne and Beatrice called. Logan and I had a turn on our bicycles. Finished I Chronicles in the evening. Bernhard had a neuralgic headache.

Wednesday Feb. 9.98.

Went on my bicycle to take a music lesson of Mme. Platonoff – said to be a good teacher. Called on Janet on way home. Bernhard went to Uffizi and called on Benn. Janet had a most friendly letter from Dolmetsch.
