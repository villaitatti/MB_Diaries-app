Monday Jan. 25.97. Fiesole

Really began my Louvre Guide – i.e. Bernhard did! We lunched with Mrs. Eyre and enjoyed her gossip very much. Logan and I went to Florence and shopped. I called on Janet Dodge, and we ended up at Placci’s with some music. Milton in the evening.

Tuesday Jan. 26. 1897.

Logan lunched with Mr. Hamilton and Bernhard with Herbert Horne, so I had the day to myself – and quite enjoyed my solitary walk. I printed Bernhard’s manuscript and enjoyed that, and after the “rampart” walk I called on the Jeffreasons. Horne came to tea. In the evening we went on with “Paradise Lost”.
