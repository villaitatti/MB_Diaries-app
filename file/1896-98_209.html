Friday Jan. 28. 1898. Viareggio

We “excursioned” to the Lago Massacuccioli [i.e. Massaciuccoli], through the Pineta – Mrs. Moor and I driving, and the other taking turns on their bicycles. We sailed across the lake to the Bagni Neroni. In the evening we had so many more platitudes that I was fain to retire early to bed. Moor is not quite a European, tho’ he is smeared with European commonplaces – he is like a Syrian who has educated himself on back numbers of the Daily Chronicle.
