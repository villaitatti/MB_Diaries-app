Monday Jan. 31. 1898. Frullino

Bernhard and Logan and I went to a concert and heard Haydn’s Kaiser Quartette and the Kreuzer sonata, with Buonamici playing like an angel. I then called on the Hornes and Bernhard on Miss Monroe, the Chicago poetess. Began II Samuel.

Tuesday Feb. 1. 1898.

Miss Blood, the Kerr-Lawsons and Mr. Johnson come to lunch. Afterwards I sat for my portrait, and Bernhard took Miss Blood over and showed her Duccio photographs. We were terribly bored with II Samuel in the evening!
