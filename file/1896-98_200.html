Wednesday Jan. 19.98. Frullino. Florence.

Bernhard and Horne went to look for Andrea del Castagnos – and found one. I – at last – got to work on my Louvre Guide. I joined them at lunch, and afterwards called on Maud Cruttwell. Miss Blood was here when I came back. She said the housekeeping of the Gamberaia had kept her so busy for 18 months that she had only gone two or three times to see any work of art in Florence.
