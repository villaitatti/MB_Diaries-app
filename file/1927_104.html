Saturday, September 10. Stoclet’s (303 Avenue de Terueren). Rain.

We went to Ghent and to Bruges, where the Stoclets delayed so long that we got home at 9, tired out. Very interesting however.

Sunday, September 11. Stoclet’s Brussels.

Went to lunch with the Guinottes, seeing Nivelles and the chasse de S. Gertrude
