I have had to give up the Burkes at last, that is, to recognize that I have lent them most of the characteristics which have given me sympathy with them. Mrs. B writes me tonight — after all she has written and said about the “cruelty of tearing her child from her”!! — that she never loved it, and would have parted with it anyhow, quite independent of Jack. I fancy he has driven her into a nasty corner, but I shall rather die fighting than creep out in such an ignoble way.
