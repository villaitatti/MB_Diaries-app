Monday, November 27, 1922

Rainy

Monica Kitson ill in bed. Lady K. ailing, she looked old and haggard and her talk was certainly very dull.

“wonderful people. But to write this letter I have been working like a dog, in fighting with a whole library of dictionaries and grammars. And I am tired like the Creator of nature after 6 days of his work. And I also rest like him. Blessed be his name. Amen”

G. Salvemini
