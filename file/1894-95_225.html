And yet if it had been Bernhard, should I have blamed him? Sometimes I think not, for Love in whatever form it comes is a God, and even if it destroys all one’s so-called ‘moral nature’ it remakes the world “nearer to the heart’s desire”. Why should we put faithfulness above it?

But I must not think in this way. It is my wickedness trying to make excuses for my unkind, miserable conduct.
