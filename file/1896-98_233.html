Tuesday Mar. 22.98

Called on the Scott-Barbers. They were choked with commonplaces, but their place is lovely, and they told a good story of a little girl who asked what time God dined – “Sh! dear! God doesn’t dine” – “O doesn’t he? Well then I suppose he has an egg with his tea.”

Wednesday Mar. 23.98

My music lesson, and acres of shopping! Discovered that the water of this house is infected – full of animal matter, from these wretched drains! Worked on the new edition of the Florentines.

Thursday Mar. 24.98.

Kerr-Lawson to lunch – a long sitting. Called on Mrs. Ross.
