Thursday, January 5, 1922

Fine day

Still in bed with bronchitis.

B.B. and Nicky went to Hassan with the Residency party and then Mrs. D[evonshire] muddled away the rest of the morning stupidly.

Logan has called he can’t come. I am awfully disappointed. He is slower in getting over his operation than they thought.

B.B. and N. went to Creswell, and then B.B. called in the Countess of Villamarina.
