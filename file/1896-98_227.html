Wednesday March 10. [i.e. 9.] 1898.

The Collegiata and Library, and then a charming drive-walk to Pancole to see a miracle-working Madonna by Pier Francesco.

Thursday March 11. [i.e. 10.]98. Frullino. Florence.

Miss Duff-Gordon was ill, so I stayed with her while the others went to Certaldo. Bernhard went on to Castel Fiorentino, San Miniato di Tedesco and Castel Nuovo, and joined us in the evening train.
