Monday Jan. 10. 1898. Frullino. Florence

Bernhard lunched with the Mo[o]rs, and Logan and I lunched at Doney’s, and shopped. Reading Deuteronomy.

Tuesday Jan. 11. 1898.

Still “settling in” – tappezziera, falegname, etc. Bernhard’s rooms are lovely – and nearly finished. I called on the von der Hellens. A picture – Madonna – by Matteo di Gualdo came – a dear. We began Joshua.

Wednesday Jan. 12. 1898.

Bernhard lunched with the Benns and I took the chance to have Maud Cruttwell here. She was rather nice. She said Miss Anstruther Thomson was very bitter against me, as well as against Bernhard – but not so Miss Paget. However, as they are inseparable, ’tis all one.
