Thursday March 3.98. Grand Hotel Siena

Drove and bicycled to San Galgano (23 miles away) – drive enchanting, but ruins not so grand as we hoped. A pleasant but tiring day.

Friday March 4.98. Nazionale. Volterra

Saw Colle di Val d’Elsa, and then drove here, our bicycles ignominiously packed in with the luggage. A beautiful drive.

Saturday March 5.98. Volterra
