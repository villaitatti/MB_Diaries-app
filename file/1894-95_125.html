I had a rather knock-down blow, although it ought not to have been unexpected, in realizing that the children must be inevitably trained as Catholics. I had planned before just what I could do and could not, and saw no reason to change my mind. But the actual realization of it has been stunning … or rather, I feel as if a leak had been sprung, and a lot of my life-blood were being drained off. I can’t seem to recover myself, or stop the leak, and the sky might almost as well be leaden or smashing for all the difference it makes.
