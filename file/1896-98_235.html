Monday March 29. [i.e. 28.] 1898.

Rainy, and I was very ill from an uncooked lobster we had. Finished the new edition of the Florentines.

“Ray doing splendidly” a telegram says.

Tuesday March 30. [i.e. 29.],98

Rainy still, but I drove down and did oceans of shopping.

Wednesday Mar. 31. [i.e. 30.]98.

Music lesson. Called on Miss Cruttwell. We dined with the Kerr-Lawsons and met Mrs. Arkwright, Lina and her artist friend Mr. Waterfield, the singer Mr. Price, Mr. Power and Miss Wood, who sang vulgar music with charming voices.
