Wednesday, January 12, 1916, I Tatti

Julia, Geoffrey

Splendid weather

(5 lunch, 3 dinner)

No cook but Amerigo managed somehow. I went in to the Carrels, taking Mrs. Ross to call on Miss Murray, and we then called for Miss Prestley whom I brought back to lunch. We had a glorious walk over the down for S. Clemente. Geoffrey had just heard that Marcella Michela has scarlet fever. He is sure to have caught it. It is a great disappointment. He called there and left flowers and a note, and then went to the Casa di Cura.
