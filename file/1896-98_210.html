in the Pineta morning and afternoon. We took the 4 o’clock train home. Logan and I played picquet, while Bernhard listened to the Moors’ platitudes. If only the platitudes had been truths at the same time – but we find they were the refuge of an uneducated man against the educated – the expression of spite.

Sunday Jan. 30.98. Frullino. Florence

Horne, Jenkins and Janet came to lunch. It was fairly pleasant, but certainly not worthwhile. They stayed to tea. It is too long. Bernhard and I had a little walk, but he was feeling very ill with indigestion – poor dear! Finished I Samuel.
