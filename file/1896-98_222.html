Saturday Feb. 26. 1898. Frullino. Florence

Went to Uffizi. In the evening read Song of Solomon and Ecclesiastes.

Sunday Feb. 27.98.

Alfred Austin (poet laureate!) and Horne came to lunch. Bernhard and Logan called on Miss Blood. Ecclesiastes. Trevy described his religious education – “My mother used in a sort of way to say it wouldn’t do any harm to believe a certain amount, you know.”

Monday Feb. 28.98. Grand Hotel Siena

Though it rained, we started, Logan, Bernhard, Lina Duff-Gordon, Trevy and I. Horne was afraid to come. It poured and we did not dare to go to Colle [di Val d’Elsa], but came here – and it cleared in time for us
