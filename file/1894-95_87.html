Went to the museum and saw the Böcklins. There are 3 large frescoes on the staircase, which are really dreadful. Not only without drawing, but really without Inhalt. Then, upstairs, there is a sculptural head of Medusa, a portrait of a woman, grey and green and brass-yellow, a centaur battle, an allegory of life, glaring in colour, a dead Christ, a mermaid-play, a perfectly banal, vulgar portrait of himself, an (early) Diane à la chasse, and a scene in a wood at autumn where a procession of white-robed figures is coming to worship at a flaming altar, by a pool.
