He spends every evening with Aunty — never goes anywhere or sees anyone. What a life!

I spent the day shouting half-hearted sentimentalities into her ears, and evading her religious questions. Alban was the only bright spot, He’s a good fellow. He spends every evening with Aunty — never goes anywhere or sees anyone. What a life!

Plaza Hotel, New York, Saturday, Dec. 26, 1903
