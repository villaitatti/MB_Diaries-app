As we were going out we bumped against two officials. Mutual surprise. They tried to be indignant, then assured us we had risked our lives, because glass was still falling from the roofs. Told them they must know well that when you come on purpose to see pictures you did not mind risking your life. That disarmed them, and the younger was liebenswürdig enough to offer to take us through those same halls with a lantern.

In this way we had a glimpse of two pictures that change one’s idea of their painter. One is a portrait by Rubens painted with almost the readiness, and sweat of Frans Hals.
