Tho’ an artist, his whole idea is that the Thought is everything, but by Thought in music he means Art, and by thought in literature he means ideas. But he could not see it. Logan said he had had an awful day of it before we came, for the husband and wife sat exchanging commonplaces about the advance of science and the cruelty of eating animals and of vivisection, and about how Art was meant to spread Morality among the People, as if they were inspired by some direct revelation.

Moor is very like Obrist, and his wife absolutely adores him.
