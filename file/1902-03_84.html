I Tatti, Monday, Nov. 11 [10]. 1902.

Don Guido arrived, suffering from lumbago. I put him in an easy chair and showed him photographs, while Alice and B.B. drove down to town, went to Costantini’s and had tea with Gertrude. Mrs. Ross called. Wrote to Karin. Bernhard wrote to Otto Gutekunst. Finished Le Rouge et le Noir.

Tuesday, Nov. 12 [11] 1902

Worked and corrected proof. It is really too great a bore to put down the letters I write. I have to write about 10 a day. Gronau came to lunch

But as a specimen of this category of women, she is an excellent one – and one can’t help liking her, wishing her well, and being pleased to have her about. She is not really passionate, although pleasure-loving – neither is she sentimental. She is hard and vain, but there doesn’t seem a touch of malice in her, and she is not petty or intriguing. Still, I know her very little.
